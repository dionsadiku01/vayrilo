Social, but safe

Seeing who’s at your hotel only works if everyone feels safe. Privacy is built into Vayrilo from day one.

  • Verified profiles

    Every account is checked with ID and selfie.

  • Booked guests only

    Only people staying at the same hotel on overlapping dates see each other.

  • You choose if you’re visible

    Turn on invisible mode at any time, even mid-trip.

  • Numbers before names

    Before you book, you see how many are coming, never who.

  • Report and block

    Moderated chats with one-tap reporting.

  • Adults only

    Vayrilo is 18+. Age is checked during verification.

Questions

Is Vayrilo a dating app?+

No. Vayrilo helps you find a crew for your holiday: group chats, events and shared plans with people at your hotel.

How do I book a hotel?+

Directly with the hotel. Vayrilo links to the hotel’s own booking page and confirms your stay with your booking confirmation.

Who can see that I’m staying at a hotel?+

Only verified Vayrilo users with a confirmed stay at the same hotel on overlapping dates, and only if you turned visibility on. Hotels never share guest lists with us.

Is Vayrilo free for travelers?+

Yes. Joining the waitlist and using the app costs travelers nothing.
